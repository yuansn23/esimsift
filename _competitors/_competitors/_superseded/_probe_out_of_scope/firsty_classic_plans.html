Questions about Classic?

We know, it sounds too good to be true. But it’s real. Here’s how.

What is Firsty Classic?

Firsty Classic is a prepaid eSIM data bundle from 0.5 GB to 50 GB, priced the same in every country and valid for 30 days after activation. You pick the size you need, install the eSIM, and use it wherever you travel without paying local roaming rates.

How much data can I buy with Firsty Classic?

Bundles range from 0.5 GB up to 50 GB. Larger bundles come with a bulk discount, so the per-GB price drops as the pack size goes up. You can also stack top-ups if you need more mid-trip.

Does Firsty Classic really cost the same in every country?

Yes. A Classic bundle costs the same whether you activate it in Japan, Germany, Brazil or the United States. There are no country surcharges and no dynamic pricing based on where you connect.

Where can I use Firsty Classic?

Firsty Classic works in 176 countries across Europe, Asia, the Americas, Africa, Oceania and the Middle East. Popular destinations covered include the United States, United Kingdom, Japan, Thailand, Australia, Brazil, Turkey and the United Arab Emirates, along with smaller markets like Andorra, Kosovo and the Faroe Islands.

What speed does Firsty Classic run at?

Classic runs at the highest speed available on the local network we route through, typically 4G LTE or 5G depending on the country. There's no artificial throttle applied to your data.

What happens if I run out of Firsty Classic data?

You can top up in seconds through the app, either by buying another Classic bundle or switching to Unlimited for the rest of your trip. Any unused data on your current bundle keeps working until the 30 days are up.

How is Firsty Classic different from carrier roaming?

Firsty Classic charges one flat price for your bundle worldwide, while carrier roaming typically applies different rates by country or by day, often with overage fees. Everything runs through the app with no physical SIM swap, and there's no auto-renewal after your 30 days end.

Is my phone compatible with Firsty?

Firsty works on any eSIM-compatible phone, which covers iPhones from the XR onwards and most Android flagships from the last five years. To check your specific phone, dial *#06# on your keypad and look for an EID number, or visit our supported devices page.

Is there a contract or commitment required?

No, there is no contract or commitment required. You can use Firsty Classic without any obligations or commitments.
