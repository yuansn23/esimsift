Frequently Asked Questions

What is an eSIM?

An eSIM (embedded SIM) is a digital SIM that allows you to activate a cellular plan from your carrier without the need for a physical SIM card. Ideal for international travelers, eSIMs enable you to switch between carriers and data plans easily, no more juggling multiple SIM cards or facing expensive roaming charges. With an eSIM, you can conveniently manage your connectivity, ensuring seamless access to the internet wherever your travels take you.

Is BNESIM the best eSIM provider?

At BNESIM, we believe we stand out as a leading eSIM provider thanks to our distinctive approach to connectivity. Unlike many competitors, we offer lifetime data plans that never expire, allowing you to use any leftover data balance for future trips.

Additionally, we provide affordable rates and a comprehensive suite of communication services that go beyond traditional offerings. These include VPN access, international calls and phone numbers, both eSIM and physical SIM options, WiFi hotspots, and extensive enterprise features.

All these services are designed to provide seamless connectivity and enhanced communication options, making BNESIM a top choice for global travelers and businesses alike.

Is my device eSIM-compatible?

Most modern smartphones, including the latest iPhones, Google Pixel, and Samsung Galaxy models, support eSIM. Our website has a complete list of supported devices, and you can check your phone’s specifications to see if it’s eSIM-compatible.

How can I top up my eSIM?

To top up your eSIM, simply download the BNESIM eSIM app, navigate to the “My eSIMs” section, choose the eSIM you want to recharge, select the desired country for data, choose your preferred data plan, and you’re all set.

Can I make calls or send SMS with my eSIM?

While our travel eSIMs are designed primarily for data usage and do not support traditional voice calls or SMS, you can still make calls and send messages using internet-based services that rely on data for communication. Beyond just eSIMs, BNESIM offers a comprehensive suite of services including voice calls, phone numbers, and many other features available through our master app. This makes BNESIM much more than just an eSIM provider, ensuring you stay connected no matter where your travels take you.

Can I use both my SIM card and BNESIM eSIM together?

Yes, you can. Use your SIM card for calls and SMS, and the BNESIM eSIM for data.

Can I share my BNESIM data with other devices?

Yes, you can use your BNESIM eSIM for tethering or setting up a personal hotspot, as long as your data plan and device support this feature. Just enable the “Personal Hotspot” option in your device settings, and you can share your internet with other devices.

What is BNESIM’s non-expiring data?
BNESIM offers non-expiring data plans, allowing users to keep unused data without worrying about expiration dates. Unlike traditional plans, your data remains available as long as your account is active.
What if I have trouble setting up my eSIM?

Our 24/7 customer support is ready to assist! Contact us via live chat or email at support@bnesim.com.
