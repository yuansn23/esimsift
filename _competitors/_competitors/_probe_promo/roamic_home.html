An eSIM is an embedded SIM card that allows you to activate a cellular plan without needing a physical SIM card. With Roamic, you can purchase your eSIM online, scan the provided QR code, and instantly activate your data plan, enabling seamless connectivity in over 190 countries.

To install the Roamic eSIM, simply purchase your eSIM plan online. You will receive a QR code via email. Scan this code with your eSIM-compatible device to activate the plan. Detailed installation instructions are provided to ensure a smooth setup process.

Roamic eSIMs are compatible with most eSIM-enabled devices, including the latest models of smartphones, tablets, and wearables. Please check our compatibility list on the website or consult your device's specifications to confirm eSIM support.
