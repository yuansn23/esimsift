Strictly Necessary Cookies

Always Active

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually only set in response to actions made by you which amount to a request for services, such as setting your privacy preferences, logging in or filling in forms. You can set your browser to block or alert you about these cookies, but some parts of the site will not then work. These cookies do not store any personally identifiable information.

Performance and Analytics

These cookies enable the website to provide enhanced functionality, personalisation, and measure the performance of our site. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages. These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources, helping us to understand which pages are the most and least popular and see how visitors navigate the site. All information collected by these cookies is aggregated and therefore anonymous. If you do not allow these cookies, some or all of our services may not function properly, and we will not be able to monitor the site's performance or know when you have visited our site.

Targeted Advertising and Social Media

These cookies may be set through our site either by us, our advertising partners, or social media services we've added. They are designed to build a profile of your interests, either to show you relevant adverts on other sites or to allow you to share our content on social media platforms. While they don't store personal information directly, they work by uniquely identifying your browser and internet device. As a result, they can track your browser across different sites, shaping the content and messages you encounter elsewhere. Declining these cookies may result in less targeted advertising and could prevent the use or visibility of certain sharing tools on our site.
