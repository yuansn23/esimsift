About Ubigi

We track Ubigi as a top choice for travel connectivity. They offer a 50% discount through their referral program and consistent promo codes for first-time users. Unlike many eSIM providers that require a new installation for every trip, Ubigi uses one reusable profile across 200+ destinations. This makes their prepaid data plans convenient for frequent flyers.

Sells

Global eSIM data plans, connected car connectivity, laptop and tablet data bundles, regional travel SIMs, and business mobility solutions.

Best For

International travelers and digital nomads who use eSIM-compatible Windows laptops or visit multiple countries in one trip.

Known For

Ubigi is known for high-speed 5G connectivity backed by NTT and its pre-installed integration in premium vehicles like BMW and Jaguar.

Price Range

Mid-Range

Good to Know

Ubigi provides data only and doesn't include a phone number for traditional calls or SMS. Their 'unlimited' plans are subject to fair usage throttling after specific data thresholds.

How Do I Apply a Promo Code on Ubigi During Checkout?

  1. Choose a data plan: Head over to ubigi.com and select an eSIM data plan for your destination. Click 'Buy Now' to continue to checkout.
  2. Locate the Promo code box: There is a 'Promo code' box underneath the order summary.
  3. Paste the promo code: Copy a valid promo code from DontPayFull and enter it into the 'Promo code' box and click 'Apply'. If it's valid, the discount will be applied immediately.
  4. Pay for your order: Enter your payment and billing information, and click 'Pay' to complete your order.

Why is my Ubigi coupon not working?

  • Verify data plan compatibility: Some coupons work only with selected data plans. If the discount doesn't work, make sure that the plan and the coupon are a match.
  • Select the right country: Ubigi provides country-specific discounts. If the coupon doesn't apply, verify if your country or region is eligible for the deal.
  • Check esim activation status: Some offers require a working eSIM before you can use the discount. If your eSIM has not been activated, follow the setup and try again.
  • No multiple discounts: Ubigi do not allow any stack discounts, so if there is one already, remove it and reapply the coupon code.

How to Save at Ubigi

  1. Unlock the Welcome Discount: If you're a new customer, you can grab a 10% discount on your first data plan by entering the welcome promo code at checkout. Just keep in mind that this offer works for almost everything in the catalog, but excludes monthly recurring plans.
  2. Stack Referral Rewards for 50% Off:

    Once you've made your first top-up in the Ubigi app, you can share a personal referral code. This gives your friends 20% off their first purchase, and for every friend who signs up, you'll earn a coupon for 50% off your next one-off data plan.

  3. Verify Student Status for 20% Off:

    Students can verify their status through Student Beans to snag a 20% discount code. You can use this code at checkout, though it can't be combined with referral rewards or other active promotions.

  4. Avoid Website Referral Restrictions: Referral codes only work for in-app purchases, not on the main website. We suggest installing your eSIM profile first, then heading to the app to finish your first transaction so the 20% friend discount applies.
  5. Choose One-Off Plans for Initial Savings:

    You can't buy a monthly recurring plan for your first purchase since those require an account and a saved credit card. To make sure your promo code works on that first order, just pick a one-off data plan from the catalog instead.

Does Ubigi offer first-time user discounts?

Yes, Ubigi provides promo codes that offer discounts on your first eSIM data plan purchase. Discounts vary from 10% to 20%.

Can I use more than one Ubigi promo code at a time?

The service only allows one promo code to be used per order. However, you can use a discount code with promotional pricing on a data plan if one is available.

Are there any exclusive discounts for regular travellers?

Yes, they have multi-country and regional eSIM plans which work out better value for regular travellers than single country plans. With these plans, you can save up to 30% on data.

How often does Ubigi come out with new promo codes?

Is there a referral scheme or loyalty discounts at Ubigi?

No, there is no referral scheme or loyalty discounts at Ubigi as yet. However, regular customers can get subscription deals with reduced prices per GB.

Where can I find the latest Ubigi discount codes?

You can find the latest promo codes on DontPayFull, as well as via their official email newsletters and the occasional social media offer.

Are there any bulk or long-term eSIM deals?

Longer term plans and higher data packages cost less per GB. Some regional plans can save you up to 25% compared to single country plans.
