Every "unlimited data" eSIM carries a fair-use policy (FUP) — a threshold or heuristic that can slow your connection long before the calendar runs out. Providers rarely hide these policies, but they do bury them. This audit pulls the fair-use note from every unlimited plan in our database, quotes it verbatim, and grades each provider on whether its listings actually state one. If you only remember one thing: **unlimited is a billing term, not a speed promise** — see [the audit table](#audit) for what your provider says.
