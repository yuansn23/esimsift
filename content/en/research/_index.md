Every number in this section is computed, not written. The league tables below are rebuilt from the same dated snapshot of real prepaid plans that powers our [country comparisons](/compare/) — {{< count-countries >}} destinations, {{< count-providers >}} providers, thousands of plans, one sort.

That matters because most eSIM rankings you'll meet online are marketing copy wearing a table skin: one affiliate feed, republished with different fonts. We re-collect list prices from each provider, normalize them into dollars per gigabyte and dollars per day, and quote fair-use fine print verbatim rather than paraphrasing it away. When a provider quietly caps an unlimited plan, that fact ends up in the [fair-use audit](/research/fair-use-audit/), not under a rug.

Use it the way you'd use any reference shelf — start from the question you actually have. The [price index](/research/esim-price-index/) answers where data is cheapest, the fair-use audit answers what unlimited really means, and the [unlimited data study](/research/unlimited-esim/) answers what a day of data should cost. Travelers who want one number for their own dates can jump straight to the [trip calculator](/tools/); anyone who wants to see the plumbing can read [how we collect prices](/methodology/).
