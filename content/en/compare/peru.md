eSIM prices in Peru are less flat than the headline number suggests. For Peru, the lowest price on file is roamic's 1GB / 7 Days at $3.00. Put the same $3.00 through the $1.30/GB plan and you would clear about 2.3GB. roamic takes the per-gigabyte race with a 50GB / 30-day plan at $1.30/GB. Across {{< count-providers >}} providers the board runs to 113 plans for Peru, 53 sold as unlimited.

Unlimited opens at $2.27/day from alosim. The crossover in Peru is roughly 1.7GB a day, where unlimited catches the $1.30/GB buckets. Road trips beyond Lima are where network choice starts to matter more than price. Local prepaid SIMs are easy to buy in Peru, so the eSIM case rests on price and on being online the moment you land.

Peru is served by Claro, Movistar and Entel, all 5G. Expect between 10 and 150 Mbps in Peru, with the top of that range only on Claro. Claro covers the widest ground, so it is the default for anything beyond Lima. Movistar is the softer option, which matters only once you leave the main cities. Trips that continue into Colombia need a separate plan, because coverage stops at the border.
