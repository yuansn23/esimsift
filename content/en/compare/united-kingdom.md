The cheapest plan in the UK is rarely the cheapest trip. yesim leads on entry price in the UK with 500MB / 1 Day at $0.51. Spent at $0.44/GB instead, that same $0.51 would buy nearer 1.2GB. Value leadership sits with roamic, whose 50GB / 30-day plan lands at $0.44/GB. The full board is 192 plans for the UK from {{< count-providers >}} providers, 90 of them unlimited.

Unlimited opens at $0.87/day from ubigi. At $0.44/GB metered, unlimited in the UK needs about 2.0GB a day to make sense. Two weeks based in London with regular video calls is the point where a larger bucket beats a daily plan. Buying local in the UK is easy, which leaves price and instant setup as the deciding factors.

Local coverage comes from EE, Vodafone, O2 and Three in the UK, all 5G. Measured performance in the UK ranges from about 15 Mbps upward, topping out near 300. Anything that leaves London is better off on EE, which holds the widest measured range. O2 sits at the other end, fine in the cities of the UK but the first to thin out outside them. Ireland is often on the same route, so price a second plan or a regional eSIM to cover both.
