Pricing across the providers tracked in Qatar splits along one line, and it is not brand. Entry pricing starts at $0.57, with Yesim's 500MB / 1 Day. Spent at $0.98/GB instead, that same $0.57 would buy nearer 596MB. The best rate here is Nomad's 50GB / 30-day plan at $0.98/GB. {{< count-providers >}} providers list 160 plans for Qatar here, 89 of them unlimited.

For Qatar, the daily unlimited floor is $2.87 with Roamic. With metered data at $0.98/GB, unlimited starts paying only above roughly 2.9GB a day. Anyone working remotely from Doha should price the daily unlimited tier before committing. Local SIMs in Qatar are straightforward to buy, so the eSIM argument is one of price and immediate activation.

Qatar runs on Ooredoo and Vodafone Qatar, all 5G. Practical speeds span 20 to 350 Mbps across the networks. Ooredoo covers the widest ground, so it is the default for anything beyond Doha. The trade-off is Vodafone Qatar, which holds up in town and less so in between. Trips that continue into the United Arab Emirates need a separate plan, because coverage stops at the border.
