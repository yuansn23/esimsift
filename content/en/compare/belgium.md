Ranking eSIMs for Belgium by entry price gets the order wrong. yesim leads on entry price in Belgium with 500MB / 1 Day at $0.51. Spent at $0.40/GB instead, that same $0.51 would buy nearer 1.3GB. Cheapest by the gigabyte is roamic, with 50GB over 30 days at $0.40/GB. Counting everything, {{< count-providers >}} providers list 187 plans for Belgium, of which 87 are unlimited.

In Belgium, unlimited starts at $1.10 a day with roamic. Travel in Belgium that stays under 2.8GB a day is cheaper on a bucket at $0.40/GB. Streaming and hotspot sharing is what pushes a Brussels trip toward the unlimited tier. Picking up a local SIM in Belgium is simple, leaving price and immediate activation as the reasons to choose an eSIM.

Service in Belgium is provided by Proximus, Orange and Base, all 5G. Typical speeds in Belgium run from 15 to 300 Mbps on Proximus, less on the rest. Anything that leaves Brussels is better off on Proximus, which holds the widest measured range. Base is the softer option, which matters only once you leave the main cities. the Netherlands is often on the same route, so price a second plan or a regional eSIM to cover both.
