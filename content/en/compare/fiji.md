Fiji's eSIM market splits cleanly into two pricing models. Metered plans — Roami down to $2.99 for 1GB — charge for a data bucket, while Holafly sells a single shape: unlimited data billed at roughly $4.15/day. On pure $/GB, Roami wins outright: 50GB / 30 Days works out to $0.86/GB, the best rate of the 51 plans we track.

The crossover point decides it. At $4.15/day, two weeks of Holafly runs $44+, while a 20GB bucket from the same table covers a heavy two-week trip for less. Light users — maps, messaging, the odd lookup — can stop at a 3-5GB bucket and stay in single digits. Rule of thumb for Fiji: under a week and Wi-Fi at the hotel, buy the smallest bucket; remote work or hotspot sharing pushes every metered plan toward the unlimited tier.

Network-wise, Fiji runs on Vodafone Fiji, Digicel Fiji — coverage differences between them matter more than price differences between providers. Vodafone covers the Mamanucas; Digicel wins the outer islands. Check the fair-use column before buying any 'unlimited' label, and re-check this page before your trip: prices move.
