Providers in Macao price the same gigabyte very differently. Nothing in Macao comes in under yesim's Macau at $0.51. That $0.51 buys a fraction of what it would at the $0.54/GB rate, which is nearer 967MB. The best rate here is roamic's 50GB / 30 Days plan at $0.54/GB. That is 51 unlimited plans among 111 options for Macao from {{< count-providers >}} providers.

Unlimited pricing in Macao floors at $1.47/day from roamic. Compared with $0.54/GB on the bucket side, you have to clear about 2.7GB a day before unlimited is cheaper. Anyone working remotely from Macau Peninsula should price the daily unlimited tier before committing. Local prepaid SIMs are easy to buy in Macao, so the eSIM case rests on price and on being online the moment you land.

Macao is served by CTM, 3 Macau and China Telecom, all 5G. Real-world throughput lands between 20 and 300 Mbps depending on which network you land on. Away from Macau Peninsula, CTM is the network to prefer. 3 Macau sits at the other end, fine in the cities of Macao but the first to thin out outside them. Coverage in Macao will not follow you into Hong Kong, so budget for a second plan or a regional eSIM.
