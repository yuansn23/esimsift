Sort the table for Taiwan by cost per gigabyte and the order changes. The cheapest plan on the board is yesim's 500MB / 1 Day at $0.57. Put the same $0.57 through the $0.56/GB plan and you would clear about 1.0GB. roamic wins on rate, at $0.56/GB for a 50GB / 30-day bucket. Counting everything, {{< count-providers >}} providers list 127 plans for Taiwan, of which 62 are unlimited.

yesim holds the cheapest unlimited rate in Taiwan at $1.47/day. Measured against $0.56/GB, the break-even sits near 2.6GB a day. Streaming and hotspot sharing is what pushes a Taipei trip toward the unlimited tier. Nothing stops you buying a local SIM in Taiwan at the airport, which makes price and activation timing the real differentiators.

Behind every eSIM for Taiwan sit Chunghwa Telecom, Taiwan Mobile and FarEasTone, all 5G. Expect between 20 and 300 Mbps in Taiwan, with the top of that range only on Chunghwa Telecom. Anything that leaves Taipei is better off on Chunghwa Telecom, which holds the widest measured range. Taiwan Mobile is the softer option, which matters only once you leave the main cities.
