The market in Vietnam looks mid-market on entry price and much better on value. 300MB / 1 Day from yesim is the cheapest buy in Vietnam at $0.51. In Vietnam, the same $0.51 reaches about 870MB at the $0.60/GB rate. Per-gigabyte pricing is set by roamic at $0.60 for 50GB over 30 days. Between {{< count-providers >}} providers there are 153 plans for Vietnam on file, 88 carrying an unlimited label.

The cheapest unlimited plan in Vietnam comes from roamic at $1.83 a day. Daily demand above 3.0GB is what makes unlimited worth it in Vietnam, against $0.60/GB metered. Hotel Wi-Fi in Hanoi does most of the heavy lifting, which keeps metered plans cheap. ID rules apply to prepaid SIMs in Vietnam but not to travel eSIMs, which activate without paperwork.

Behind every eSIM for Vietnam sit Viettel, Vinaphone and Mobifone, all 5G. Expect between 15 and 250 Mbps in Vietnam, with the top of that range only on Viettel. Viettel posts the widest range, which is why plans riding it are the safer pick once your route leaves Hanoi. Vinaphone trails the field on range, which in Vietnam is only a problem off the beaten track. Crossing into Thailand is common, and no single plan covers both, so budget for a second plan or a regional one.
