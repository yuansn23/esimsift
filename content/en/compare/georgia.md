The market in Georgia rewards a little arithmetic. Entry pricing starts at $0.51, with Yesim's 300MB / 1 Day. Spent at $0.93/GB instead, that same $0.51 would buy nearer 565MB. By rate, Jetpac sets the floor at $0.93/GB for 40GB. The tracked catalogue holds 152 plans for Georgia from {{< count-providers >}} providers, 81 of them unlimited.

Unlimited by the day in Georgia starts at $1.60 via Roamic. Compared with $0.93/GB on the bucket side, you have to clear about 1.7GB a day before unlimited is cheaper. Travelling between Tbilisi and Batumi keeps daily usage moderate, so metered data stays the cheaper shape. Local prepaid SIMs are easy to buy in Georgia, so the eSIM case rests on price and on being online the moment you land.

Georgia is served by Magti, Silknet and Beeline, a mix of 4G and 5G. Real-world throughput lands between 8 and 150 Mbps depending on which network you land on. Away from Tbilisi, Magti is the network to prefer. Beeline sits at the other end, fine in the cities of Georgia but the first to thin out outside them. Coverage in Georgia will not follow you into Turkiye, so budget for a second plan or a regional eSIM.
