Cheap entry prices in Mexico hide a wide spread once you normalise for data. In Mexico, Ubigi 500MB / 2 Days is as cheap as it gets at $3.00. Entry plans in Mexico run about 6.8 times the best per-gigabyte rate. On cost per gigabyte Airalo leads with a 50GB / 30-day plan at $0.90/GB. {{< count-providers >}} providers list 189 plans for Mexico here, 96 of them unlimited.

Jetpac holds the cheapest unlimited rate in Mexico at $1.88/day. Against $0.90/GB for metered data, unlimited only wins past about 2.1GB a day. A city break built around Mexico City rarely clears a few gigabytes, so a small bucket is usually enough. Registration is required for prepaid SIMs in Mexico, so arriving with an eSIM already installed saves a stop.

The national networks are Telcel, AT&T Mexico and Movistar, all 5G. Throughput in Mexico sits in a 10-250 Mbps band, network permitting. Telcel covers the widest ground, so it is the default for anything beyond Mexico City. The trade-off is Movistar, which holds up in town and less so in between. Trips that continue into the USA need a separate plan, because coverage stops at the border.
