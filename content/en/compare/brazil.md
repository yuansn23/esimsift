Pricing across the providers tracked in Brazil splits along one line, and it is not brand. Nothing in Brazil comes in under Yesim's 199MB / 1 Day at $0.51. That $0.51 buys a fraction of what it would at the $0.90/GB rate, which is nearer 580MB. By rate, Nomad sets the floor at $0.90/GB for 50GB over 30 days. {{< count-providers >}} providers list 180 plans for Brazil here, 97 of them unlimited.

The cheapest unlimited plan in Brazil comes from Ubigi at $2.30 a day. Against $0.90/GB for metered data, unlimited only wins past about 2.6GB a day. Travelling between Sao Paulo and Rio de Janeiro keeps daily usage moderate, so metered data stays the cheaper shape. There is no registration barrier in Brazil, so price and setup speed are what separate an eSIM from a local SIM.

The national networks are Vivo, Claro and TIM, all 5G. Throughput in Brazil sits in a 15-250 Mbps band, network permitting. Vivo covers the widest ground, so it is the default for anything beyond Sao Paulo. The trade-off is TIM, which holds up in town and less so in between. Trips that continue into Argentina need a separate plan, because coverage stops at the border.
