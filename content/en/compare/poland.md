The cheapest unlimited plan in Poland comes from Roamic at $1.00 a day. In Poland, the metered rate of $0.36/GB puts the unlimited tipping point near 2.8GB a day. Travelling between Warsaw and Krakow keeps daily usage moderate, so metered data stays the cheaper shape. There is no registration barrier in Poland, so price and setup speed are what separate an eSIM from a local SIM.

The national networks are Orange, Play, Plus and T-Mobile, all 5G. Throughput in Poland sits in a 20-300 Mbps band, network permitting. Away from Warsaw, Orange is the network to prefer. Play sits at the other end, fine in the cities of Poland but the first to thin out outside them. Coverage in Poland will not follow you into Germany, so budget for a second plan or a regional eSIM.
