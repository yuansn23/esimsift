In the Philippines, unlimited starts at $2.06 a day with yesim. Set against the best $0.56/GB metered rate, the daily plan needs roughly 3.7GB a day to pay off. Streaming and hotspot sharing is what pushes a Manila trip toward the unlimited tier. Local prepaid SIMs in the Philippines come with registration requirements that travel eSIMs do not.

Service in the Philippines is provided by Globe and Smart, all 5G. Typical speeds in the Philippines run from 15 to 200 Mbps on Globe, less on the rest. Globe posts the widest range, which is why plans riding it are the safer pick once your route leaves Manila. Smart trails the field on range, which in the Philippines is only a problem off the beaten track. Crossing into Indonesia is common, and no single plan covers both, so budget for a second plan or a regional one.
