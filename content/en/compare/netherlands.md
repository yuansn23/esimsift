The market in the Netherlands rewards a little arithmetic. yesim holds the entry price with 500MB / 1 Day at $0.51. That gap is the story in the Netherlands in one line: 2.9 times the rate for the smallest bucket. roamic wins on rate, at $0.36/GB for a 50GB / 30-day bucket. The tracked catalogue holds 184 plans for the Netherlands from {{< count-providers >}} providers, 88 of them unlimited.

In the Netherlands, unlimited starts at $1.00 a day with roamic. Below about 2.8GB a day, buckets in the Netherlands at $0.36/GB stay ahead of any daily plan. A week split between Amsterdam and Rotterdam is where the small-bucket advice stops holding. Local SIMs in the Netherlands are straightforward to buy, so the eSIM argument is one of price and immediate activation.

The Netherlands runs on KPN, Vodafone and Odido, all 5G. Measured performance in the Netherlands ranges from about 20 Mbps upward, topping out near 300. Away from Amsterdam, KPN is the network to prefer. Vodafone sits at the other end, fine in the cities of the Netherlands but the first to thin out outside them. Coverage in the Netherlands will not follow you into Belgium, so budget for a second plan or a regional eSIM.
