eSIM prices in the USA are less flat than the headline number suggests. Nothing in the USA comes in under yesim's 500MB / 1 Day at $0.51. That $0.51 buys a fraction of what it would at the $0.62/GB rate, which is nearer 842MB. On cost per gigabyte alosim leads with a 50GB / 10-day plan at $0.62/GB. Across {{< count-providers >}} providers the board runs to 220 plans for the USA, 91 sold as unlimited.

In the USA, unlimited starts at $1.67 a day with roami. With metered data at $0.62/GB, unlimited starts paying only above roughly 2.7GB a day. A city break built around New York rarely clears a few gigabytes, so a small bucket is usually enough. Local SIMs in the USA are straightforward to buy, so the eSIM argument is one of price and immediate activation.

The USA runs on T-Mobile, AT&T and Verizon, all 5G. Practical speeds span 25 to 350 Mbps across the networks. Verizon covers the widest ground, so it is the default for anything beyond New York. The trade-off is T-Mobile, which holds up in town and less so in between. Trips that continue into Canada need a separate plan, because coverage stops at the border.
