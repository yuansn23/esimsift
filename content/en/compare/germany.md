The cheapest plan in Germany is rarely the cheapest trip. For the smallest outlay you get Yesim's 500MB / 1 Day at $0.51. That $0.51 buys a fraction of what it would at the $0.50/GB rate, which is nearer 1.0GB. The value crown is aloSIM's, at $0.50/GB for 50GB over 10 days. The unlimited tier starts at $1.00 a day from Roamic. Travel in Germany that stays under 2.0GB a day is cheaper on a bucket at $0.50/GB. If the trip stays inside Berlin and Munich, a modest bucket covers maps, messaging and the odd booking. Picking up a local SIM in Germany is simple, leaving price and immediate activation as the reasons to choose an eSIM.

Service in Germany is provided by Telekom, Vodafone and O2, all 5G. Typical speeds in Germany run from 15 to 300 Mbps on Telekom, less on the rest. Anything that leaves Berlin is better off on Telekom, which holds the widest measured range. O2 is the softer option, which matters only once you leave the main cities. France is often on the same route, so price a second plan or a regional eSIM to cover both.
