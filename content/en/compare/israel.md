Two pricing models compete in Israel, and they suit different trips. Cheapest of the lot is yesim 500MB / 1 Day at $0.57. In Israel, the same $0.57 reaches about 1.1GB at the $0.50/GB rate. roamic takes the per-gigabyte race with a 50GB / 30-day plan at $0.50/GB. The tracked catalogue holds 161 plans for Israel from {{< count-providers >}} providers, 88 of them unlimited.

Unlimited prices better than it looks: roamic undercuts the field at $1.37 a day. In Israel, the metered rate of $0.50/GB puts the unlimited tipping point near 2.7GB a day. Road trips beyond Tel Aviv are where network choice starts to matter more than price. Registration is required for prepaid SIMs in Israel, so arriving with an eSIM already installed saves a stop.

The national networks are Cellcom, Partner and Pelephone, all 5G. Throughput in Israel sits in a 20-300 Mbps band, network permitting. Away from Tel Aviv, Cellcom is the network to prefer. Pelephone sits at the other end, fine in the cities of Israel but the first to thin out outside them.
