Two pricing models compete in Singapore, and they suit different trips. The cheapest option in Singapore is yesim 500MB / 1 Day at $0.51. Small buckets cost roughly 2.6 times the rate of the big ones here. On cost per gigabyte roamic leads with a 50GB / 30-day plan at $0.40/GB. The tracked catalogue holds 154 plans for Singapore from {{< count-providers >}} providers, 88 of them unlimited.

Priced per day, unlimited bottoms out at $1.43 with roamic. Compared with $0.40/GB on the bucket side, you have to clear about 3.6GB a day before unlimited is cheaper. A city break built around Orchard Road rarely clears a few gigabytes, so a small bucket is usually enough. Local prepaid SIMs are easy to buy in Singapore, so the eSIM case rests on price and on being online the moment you land.

Singapore is served by Singtel, StarHub and M1, all 5G. Real-world throughput lands between 20 and 400 Mbps depending on which network you land on. Away from Orchard Road, Singtel is the network to prefer. M1 sits at the other end, fine in the cities of Singapore but the first to thin out outside them. Coverage in Singapore will not follow you into Malaysia, so budget for a second plan or a regional eSIM.
