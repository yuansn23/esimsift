Entry plans in Hong Kong run about 2.2 times the best per-gigabyte rate. Value leadership sits with roamic, whose 50GB / 30-day plan lands at $0.52/GB. Unlimited opens at $1.41/day from roamic. Since buckets cost $0.52/GB, the daily plan only makes sense past about 2.7GB a day. Two weeks based in Central with regular video calls is the point where a larger bucket beats a daily plan. Buying local in Hong Kong is easy, which leaves price and instant setup as the deciding factors.

Local coverage comes from CSL, 3HK and SmarTone in Hong Kong, all 5G. Measured performance in Hong Kong ranges from about 20 Mbps upward, topping out near 400. CSL posts the widest range, which is why plans riding it are the safer pick once your route leaves Central. 3HK trails the field on range, which in Hong Kong is only a problem off the beaten track. Crossing into Taiwan is common, and no single plan covers both, so budget for a second plan or a regional one.
