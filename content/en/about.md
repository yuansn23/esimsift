## Why eSIM Sift exists

Buying a travel eSIM in 2026 is harder than it should be. Every provider runs its own store, its own pricing structure and its own definition of "unlimited" - and every review site seems to be selling something. We built eSIM Sift to answer one question honestly: **for the country you are visiting and the data you will actually use, which plan costs least?**

We do that with a maintained price database rather than opinions. Every plan on this site is a real, current listing from a real provider, re-checked on a fixed schedule, with the derived metrics - price per GB, price per day, fair-use limits - computed the same way for every provider.

## What we are not

We are not a reseller. We do not host plans or process orders. When you click through to a provider, you buy from them directly.

We are also not a neutral as-a-matter-of-ownership: eSIM Sift and the Roami eSIM app share the same owner, and we disclose that openly [here](/disclosure/). What we can promise is that the comparison tables are generated from the same database with the same rules for every provider, Roami included - where Roami loses on price, the table says so.
