## What we collect

eSIM Sift is a content site: we do not require accounts and we do not process payments. We use privacy-respecting analytics to understand which pages are useful. Outbound clicks to providers are measured in aggregate.

## Cookies

Essential cookies only, plus analytics unless you opt out. No advertising cookies are set by this site.

## Third parties

When you leave eSIM Sift through an outbound link, the destination provider's own privacy policy applies.
