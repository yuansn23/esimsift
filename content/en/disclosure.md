## Affiliate relationships

eSIM Sift earns referral commissions from some of the providers listed on this site. When you click certain outbound links and buy a plan, we may receive a commission at no additional cost to you. Links that carry commission are marked with `rel="sponsored"` in this page's HTML.

## The rules that keep the comparisons honest

1. **Every provider appears in the same tables under the same rules.** Its badges, sort positions and prices come from the identical database pipeline as every other provider.
2. **Where a provider loses, the table shows it.** Pick any page: if another provider is cheaper for a plan size, that provider carries the badge.
3. **Provider pages list weaknesses for everyone** — no profile on this site is promo copy.

## What we don't do

- We don't accept payment for placement or ranking order.
- We don't remove negative facts from provider profiles in exchange for commission terms.
- We don't let commission rates influence the "Best value" badge - it is a literal sort position, see the [methodology](/methodology/).
