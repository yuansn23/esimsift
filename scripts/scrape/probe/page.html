Airalo for United States of America FAQ

Does Airalo offer unlimited data eSIM for United States of America?

Airalo does not provide a truly unlimited data eSIM for the United States of America. The plans that carry the word “Unlimited” in their name actually offer 3GB of high‑speed data per day and reduce the speed to 1000kbps after that daily limit is reached, so the data is capped.

Travelers who need more data can choose from Airalo’s fixed data plans. For example, a 50GB plan is available for 30 days at $42.00 USD, and a 20GB plan is available for 15 days at $36.00 USD. These fixed data plans give a larger total data allowance and may be more cost‑effective for most users.

Does Airalo offer United States of America eSIM with phone number and SMS?

Airalo offers United States of America eSIM plans that include a phone number and the ability to send and receive calls and SMS. The available plans range from 1GB to 50GB of data and cost between $6.00 and $59.00 USD. Validity periods vary from 3 days to 30 days, so users can choose a short‑term or longer‑term option that matches their travel schedule.

All of these plans provide a local United States phone number with the dialing code +1, allow inbound and outbound voice calls, and support both inbound and outbound SMS. Users should review the specific plan details on Airalo’s website and confirm any additional requirements directly with the provider.

How many data plans does Airalo offer for United States of America?

Airalo lists 30 single‑country data plans that cover the United States and 36 multi‑country plans that include the United States within their coverage. The available plans span a price range from about $4 to $159 and offer data limits from 1 GB up to 50 GB. Some plans are fixed‑data packages that grant a set amount of data for a set validity period, while others are daily‑cap plans that provide a daily data allowance. The single‑country options focus exclusively on the United States, whereas the multi‑country packages bundle the U.S. with other regions for travelers seeking broader roaming coverage.
