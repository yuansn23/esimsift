Read the prices right

How unlimited eSIM pricing works

Per day rather than per GB

Unlimited plans sell a number of days, not gigabytes. A “$3.90/day” plan bought for 7 days costs $27.30 upfront — the daily rate is the only number that compares across trip lengths.

Longer trips mean a lower daily rate

Most providers discount longer validity. The cheapest daily rate in a country is usually the 20–30 day package, not the 5-day one — our table always shows the best rate.

“Unlimited” always has a ceiling

Every unlimited plan we track carries a fair-use policy that can throttle your speed. We record each plan's policy note verbatim — see the fair-use audit before you buy.
