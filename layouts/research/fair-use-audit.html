Before you buy

Four things to check on any unlimited plan

1 · The daily rate rather than the sticker price

Divide the price by the days. The unlimited league table does this for every country we track.

2 · Whether a fair-use note exists at all

A listing with no FUP note isn't a promise of unthrottled data — it just means the seller didn't state one. Treat “unstated” as “unknown”, not “none”.

3 · Hotspot sharing rules

Tethering is where fair-use policies bite hardest. Some plans allow it, some throttle it, some drop it after a threshold — it's in the per-plan notes on each country page.

4 · Whether a big metered bucket is cheaper

If your usage is predictable, a 20GB metered plan often beats unlimited on price. Check both tables on your destination's page before committing.
