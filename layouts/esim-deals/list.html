When a discount code still loses

A code changes what you pay — but the ranking in our tables is always the pre-code baseline. Here's the honest follow-up question: after applying each code to the provider's cheapest plan in every country, in how many of our {{ len hugo.Data.countries }} tracked countries would that provider actually become #1? Sometimes the answer is "all of them". Sometimes it's "still none — just buy the other brand."

How to redeem

How to use an eSIM promo code

  1. Step 1

    Compare first

    Open the country comparison and find the plan you actually want — a code on the wrong plan is still overpaying.

  2. Step 2

    Buy through the provider

    Buy with the "Get" button beside the code, so the code's country and eligibility match your order.

  3. Step 3

    Paste before paying

    Look for the "Promo code" or "Discount code" field at checkout (web) or in the payment sheet (app) — copy it from the card above.

  4. Step 4

    Check the new total

    The line total must drop before you pay. If it doesn't, the code is dead or your plan isn't eligible — don't retry with random codes.

  5. Step 5

    Install and go

    Install the eSIM per our step-by-step guide — codes never change how activation works.

Beyond codes

Four ways to save that beat most coupon boxes

Right provider beats 20% off the wrong one

Across our tracked countries, the gap between the cheapest and most expensive provider on the same destination regularly runs wider than any code's discount. The price index shows the spread country by country — start there, not at a coupon site.

Unlimited isn't automatically expensive

Unlimited plans win on price once your usage passes a break-even point (around 1.1 GB/day in the median country we track). Check when unlimited is cheaper before paying for data you won't use.

Match the plan to the trip instead of the headline

A 30-day bundle on a 5-day trip is money in the bin. Our data calculator and each country page's "how to choose" section size the plan to your days and habits.

Do seasonal sales beat the everyday price

Black Friday and summer sales can hit 30-50% at some providers. Our tables always show the list price, so you can tell a real discount from an inflated "was" price. Bookmarked this page is the fastest way back.
