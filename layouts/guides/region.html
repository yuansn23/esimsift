Before you buy

A regional eSIM or single country eSIM

This guide compares single-country plans country by country, because that is what the country pages show and what we can verify line by line. Regional and multi-country eSIMs exist and can be cheaper when you cross several borders in one trip — but their prices are not listed the same way, so we never mix them into the league table above. The honest rule of thumb: for one country, buy that country's cheapest single-country plan; for three or more countries, check whether a regional plan beats the sum of its single-country parts.

One destination

Buy the single-country plan that tops the table for that country. The cheapest provider there is visible in the country page's own league table.

Several destinations

Sum the cheapest single-country plans for your route first, then compare that total against any regional plan before committing.
